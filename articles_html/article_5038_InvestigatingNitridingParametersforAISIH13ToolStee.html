Investigating Nitriding Parameters for AISI H13 Tool Steel: A Microhardness Study Using Full Factorial Design

Authors

  • Umesh Patharkar Dr Babasaheb Ambedkar Marathwada University, Chatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra, India 431004 https://orcid.org/0009-0005-9303-641X
  • Sunil Patil Department of Mechanical Engineering, Government College of Engineering, Jalgaon, Maharashtra, India- 425002 https://orcid.org/0009-0000-4804-0720

DOI:

https://doi.org/10.52756/ijerr.2025.v47.014

Keywords:

AISI H13 tool steel, Full factorial method, Gas nitriding, Surface hardening

Abstract

This research investigates the parameters affecting gas nitriding to increase the surface microhardness of AISI H13 tool steel. Used full factorial experimental design testing combinations of nitriding temperature (200°C, 300°C, 400°C) with nitriding time (12, 24, 36 hours) under constant ammonia flow (0.5 L/min, atmospheric pressure conditions. The Vickers microhardness (HV?.?) test results demonstrated that hardness reaches its maximum at 400°C among temperature treatment conditions. The hardness peak value reached 1062 HV during the optimal condition at 400°C for 24 hours but embrittlement caused the hardness to decrease by 4% when the process exceeded 36 hours. Hardness reached its peak value of 952 HV during 24 hours of treatment at 300°C before reduction happened, although 200°C produced only small enhancements between 853 HV and 867 HV because of inadequate nitrogen migration. Data analysis through interaction and contour plots demonstrated temperature as the determining factor for experimental results after 24-hour processing time.
